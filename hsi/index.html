Haiti Security Index (HSI)

A text-based monthly measure of violence and threat perceptions in Haiti

📅  Last updated: May 2026

About the Haiti Security Index

The Haiti Security Index (HSI) is a monthly text-based measure of domestic security conditions spanning January 1997 to the present. It is built from systematic keyword searches of English- and French-language news articles from nine outlets — four Haitian and five international — aggregated and normalized by total media volume.

Following the methodology of Caldara and Iacoviello (2022) Geopolitical Risk Index, the HSI separately tracks realized violent acts (HSI-Acts) and forward-looking threat perceptions (HSI-Threats), as well as a composite measure (HSI-All), each normalized to a sample mean of 100. The index captures all major security episodes in Haiti's modern history: the 2004 political crisis, the 2010 earthquake, the 2021 presidential assassination, and the 2022–2025 gang consolidation period.

The HSI fills a critical data gap in IMF surveillance by providing a high-frequency, replicable, and forward-looking security indicator suitable for nowcasting GDP, inflation, exchange rate pressures, and other macroeconomic outcomes.
